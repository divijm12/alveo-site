The short version

1. The Alveo app (everyone)

What the app does with your camera

While Alveo is running and not paused, it reads frames from your computer's camera and analyzes them on your computer to find your face, your hands, and whether a vape is at your lips. Frames are processed in memory and then discarded. They are not recorded or saved, and they never leave your computer. The only exception is the private beta, where testers agree to share short mouth-area clips (section 2).

When you lock your screen or step away, Alveo turns the camera off, and it comes back when you use your keyboard or mouse. You can pause it from the menu bar (Mac) or tray icon (Windows) at any time. Your computer's camera light is on whenever the camera is in use, and no app, including Alveo, can turn that light off.

The face and hand positions Alveo finds are used in the moment to decide whether you are vaping. They are not used to recognize who you are, and Alveo doesn't keep them (beta clips include lip and hand positions, see section 2).

What the app stores, and where

Everything below stays on your computer, in ~/Library/Application Support/Alveo/ on a Mac or %LOCALAPPDATA%\Alveo\ on Windows. We don't receive any of it. The only exceptions are what private-beta testers agree to share (section 2), and optional statistics if we ever offer them and you turn them on (section 4).

2. The Alveo private beta (invited testers only)

Everything in this section happens only in the private beta. It ends when the beta ends. The private beta is invite-only and is for adults 18 and older who live in the United States, except Illinois, Washington and Texas, where we don't offer it. Every tester agrees in person to help train Alveo before getting an invite, and agrees again in the app before the beta starts. Beta builds do these extra things:

Requesting access and your invite

If you ask to join on beta.getalveo.app, we store your name, email and the optional note you write, and email the Alveo admins to review it. If you're invited, we store your invite (your name, email and a personal access code) and email you the code.

Activation and staying active

The first time you open the beta, it checks your access code and ties your invite to that one computer using a one-way scrambled ID (a hash) of the computer's hardware ID. We cannot turn that ID back into your computer's hardware ID. We store the date you agreed to the beta terms and the date you activated.

About once an hour while it runs, the beta checks with our server that your invite is still active, and reports the app's version and whether it runs on Mac or Windows. It also checks for updates and downloads them. When we revoke an invite, the app stops at its next check.

Clip sharing (training Alveo)

When you answer an alert ("Did you just vape?" or "Were you vaping?"), the beta uploads the roughly 3 seconds of small mouth-area images the app used to decide, together with your answer, the positions of your lips and hands in those images, the app version and the time.

Nothing else is uploaded: no full camera frames, nothing when you don't answer, no audio, no puff history, no screen contents.

We use clips only to train and test Alveo's vape detector. Only one member of the Alveo team can open clips, to check what the detector saw and to correct a clip's training label if an answer was clearly wrong. Correcting a label changes only how the clip is used for training, never your answer, app or counts. Clips are deleted automatically after 6 months.

A detector that has already been trained on clips can't "unlearn" them, but deleted clips are never used again.

Feedback reports

If you send feedback or a bug report from the app, we receive your message, the type of report, your app and operating-system versions, and your invite's name and email so we can reply. If you tick "Include diagnostic log", we also receive the last part of the app's log file (app messages and puff times, never camera images). Reports are deleted after 6 months.

Your choices in the beta

  • Delete my shared clips (Settings) deletes every clip you've shared and turns sharing off. The beta keeps working.
  • Delete my data (Settings) deletes everything on your computer and your shared clips and feedback on our server.
  • Email privacy@getalveo.app to delete your invite and everything linked to it.

4. Usage and progress statistics

Today, Alveo doesn't send any usage or progress statistics. Your puff counts, plan and dashboard numbers stay on your computer (the private beta's check-ins described above only report the app's version and whether it runs on Mac or Windows).

In the future we may offer an optional setting to share statistics that help us see whether Alveo works and fix problems. If we do, these rules will apply:

  • Off unless you turn it on. The app will ask you first, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Daily summaries only, such as how many puffs Alveo counted that day, your limit, and how many urge timers you finished. Never the time of each puff, and never anything about where you are.
  • Product events and crash reports, such as "finished setup" or "the app crashed", with the app and operating-system versions.
  • Not linked to your name or email. Statistics use a random ID that only the app knows.
  • Never images, video or audio.
  • Kept on our own server (Supabase), never given to analytics or advertising companies, and never used for ads.
  • You can delete them with Delete my data or by emailing us.

We will update this policy and ask for your agreement before any statistics are collected.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we have about you, give you a copy, correct it, or delete it. You can also withdraw your consent to the beta or to analytics at any time, as described above. Email privacy@getalveo.app. We'll reply within 45 days, and we may need to confirm your email address first. If we turn down a request, you can ask us to reconsider it by replying to that email. 8. Security

We use encrypted connections, private storage that only our server functions can reach, access limited to the people who need it, and signed activation tokens. No system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee that information we hold will never be accessed without permission. If that happens, we'll tell affected people as the law requires.

9. Health information and age

Alveo is a habit-awareness tool, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat or cure any condition, and its lung pictures are illustrations, not measurements of your lungs. If you want help quitting, a free quitline (1-800-QUIT-NOW in the US) or your doctor can help.

Alveo is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think someone under 18 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make a change that matters, we'll update this page and the date above. The app will show you the new version and ask you to agree before the camera starts again. We won't use information we already have in a materially different way without asking you first.
